Pickaxes mine stone fastest and hatchets chop wood fastest, but either works on both. Oak needs a Stone Hatchet or better to chop quickly.

Big Mingers (mutant rats) and Bawheids (ghouls) attack on contact. Deep underground, Peely-Wallies hunt in the dark, but they run from fire: carry a torch or stay near a Wee Fire. Falling more than 4 blocks also hurts.

Heal with Wee Berries, a Big Plaster, or a Rat Piece (raw meat cooked on a Wee Fire).

Weather changes every few minutes, and Wee Davie radios a warning first. Ash Cloud hurts you outside, so get under a roof or underground. Heavy Dreich regrows plants but puts out fires left in the open. Taps Aff makes you hungry and tired faster in the sun.

Your food bar drains slowly. Eat berries or cooked meat before it runs out, or you start to starve.

Jumping, swinging tools and fighting use stamina. Walking doesn't. Pause for a moment and it refills.

You can only mine blocks you can see. Clear anything in the way first.

Some recipes need a Workbench or Furnace placed within 4 blocks of you.
