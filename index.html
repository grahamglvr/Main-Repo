Pickaxes mine stone fastest and hatchets chop wood fastest, but either works on both. Oak needs a Stone Hatchet or better to chop quickly.

Mutant rats and ghouls attack on contact. Deep underground, pale crawlers hunt in the dark, but they run from fire: carry a torch or stay near a campfire. Falling more than 4 blocks also hurts.

Heal with berries, bandages, or raw meat cooked on a Campfire.

Jumping, swinging tools and fighting use stamina. Walking doesn't. Pause for a moment and it refills.

You can only mine blocks you can see. Clear anything in the way first.

Some recipes need a Workbench or Furnace placed within 4 blocks of you.
